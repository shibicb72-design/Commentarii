# Plan v2 - Week and Month pages (branch opus/v2-week-month, stacked on opus/v1-core)

## Goal
Add WEEKLY and MONTHLY page types to the existing Page + section-block model, with "Week" and "Month" destinations between Today and Notes. Add a read-only "This week & month" card at the top of the Daily page. The new pages take part in search, folders, tags, pins and export/import. No network, keep SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, no change to rollover.

## Root-cause notes (why a migration is needed)
- `Entities.kt:52` has a unique index on `pages.date`. A weekly page keyed by its Monday, or a monthly page keyed by the 1st, would clash with that day's DAILY page. Fix: Room v1 -> v2 with `MIGRATION_1_2`. It drops `index_pages_date` and creates a unique `index_pages_type_date(type, date)`. No new table. `app/schemas/.../1.json` already exists, so the migration can be tested.
- These DAO queries have no type filter: `search` (Daos.kt:134), `getPagesInFolder` (:156), `getPagesWithTag` (:190) and `getAllPages` (:197). That is what we want, since the new pages then show up in search, folders, tags and export. The openers in `SearchScreen.kt:109` and `AppNav.kt:200` branch on type and must learn the new types.
- Rollover uses `getLatestDailyBefore` (DAILY only), so weekly and monthly tasks are never carried over. Nothing to change.
- There is no mood range query yet, so one is added (DAO only, no schema change). Self-care already has `getChecks/observeChecks(from,to)` and `toggleCheck(itemId,date)`.

## Decisions
- A week is Monday to Sunday. The weekly page is keyed by its Monday's `date`, and the monthly page by the 1st of the month.
- Habit summary denominator: past month = days in month, current month = days elapsed including today, future month = shown as "-". Displayed as "22/30".
- A day cell counts as having content if it has a mood, non-blank task or schedule text, or non-empty notes.
- Visiting the week or month pages never creates DAILY pages. Tapping a day opens `daily/{date}`, which creates it as it does today.
- Titles are stored in English for FTS. On screen they come from strings.xml formats.
