NoteToDo Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

Summary

NoteToDo does not collect, send or share any personal data.

  • The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything over the network.
  • There is no account or sign-in.
  • There are no analytics, no tracking and no ads.

Where your data is stored

Your notes, to-dos and settings are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. Android backup is turned off for NoteToDo, so the data is not copied to Google cloud backup or moved to a new phone by device-to-device transfer. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

Export and import

When you choose to export, NoteToDo writes a backup file only to the location you pick in the Android file picker. When you import, it reads only the file you pick. NoteToDo never sends these files anywhere. An exported file is yours: it stays where you saved it, even after you uninstall the app, until you delete it.

Permissions

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): to show the reminders you set.
  • Exact alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): to show reminders at the exact time you chose.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): to set your reminders again after the phone restarts or the time or time zone changes.
  • Biometrics (USE_BIOMETRIC, and USE_FINGERPRINT for older Android versions, both added by the Android biometric library): for the optional app lock. Your fingerprint or face data stays with Android; NoteToDo only receives a yes/no result.
  • Internal app permission (com.shibi.notetodo.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION, added by an Android support library): lets the app's own components talk to each other privately. Other apps cannot use it. It gives no access to your data.

Children

NoteToDo collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
